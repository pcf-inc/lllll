ABOUT POR CRISTO FOUNDATION

Learn about our founding spiritual mission, compassionate leadership team, and rigorous resident privacy policies.

Our History & Calling

Providing Hope to Forgotten Seniors Since 2008

Por Cristo Foundation was founded as a non-profit sanctuary dedicated to abandoned, neglected, and homeless elderly citizens who have nowhere else to go.

We provide full shelter, specialized geriatric medical monitoring, physical rehabilitation, warm daily meals, and spiritual companionship in a secure home-like environment.

Our Mission

To honor Christ by caring for abandoned seniors with dignified shelter, medical treatment, unconditional love, and spiritual warmth.

Our Vision

A compassionate society where no senior citizen suffers isolation, hunger, or lack of medical care in their twilight years.

Resident Privacy & Confidentiality Safeguards

1. Elderly Resident Confidentiality: Por Cristo Foundation enforces strict safeguards regarding medical records, full legal names, and sensitive health histories of all senior residents.

2. Photo & Story Anonymization: Public-facing resident imagery is rendered through our optional Privacy Shield blur mode to protect personal dignity and legal identity.

3. Admission & Referral Data: All admission inquiries and referral submission forms are kept strictly confidential for intake evaluation by our licensed social workers.
